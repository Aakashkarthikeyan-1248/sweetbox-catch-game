🏆 How to win

①Reach 45 points before the 30-second timer ends.
②Each sweetbox gives +15, so 3 clean catches can win it.
③Negative objects reduce your score. Keep moving!

💡 Quick tip

Watch the falling lane, not just the score. The sweetbox has a golden glow. The other objects are distractions—avoid them if you can.
